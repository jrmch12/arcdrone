Teacher (privileged path):
  teacher_obs/value_obs
        |
  [Teacher Decoder MLP] -> teacher_feat
        |                        \
        |                         \---> [Teacher Action Head] -> teacher_logits
        V

Student (vision + proprio):
  pixels/view_0  --+                    
                   |--> [CNN] -> cnn_feats --+
  proprio_obs ---+                         |
                                             +-> [Fusion MLP] -> student_feat

Heads (from student_feat):
  student_feat -> [Student Action Head (trainable)] -> student_logits
  student_feat -> [Aux Vel Head (aux_vel_mlp) -> 3D vel_pred]

Ground truth and losses:
  aux_linvel (env)  -----+                        
                          +--> Aux vel loss = MSE(vel_pred, stop_grad(aux_linvel))
  stop_grad(teacher_feat) -+--> Embed loss = mean|student_feat - stop_grad(teacher_feat)|
  stop_grad(teacher_logits) -+--> Action loss = mean|student_logits - stop_grad(teacher_logits)|

Total loss used during alignment:
  total = embed_coef*embed_loss + action_coef*action_loss + aux_vel_coef*aux_vel_loss

Notes:
 - `aux_vel_mlp` is defined in src/arcdrone/New_attempt_2/training/networks.py (layers [64,32,3]).
 - Aux head is training-only; its params are included in the optimizer but not saved to final checkpoints.
 - Aux ground-truth `aux_linvel` is produced in src/arcdrone/New_attempt_2/task/obs.py.
 - Loss computed in src/arcdrone/New_attempt_2/training/align.py.
